A cannon fires a ball horizontally from a raised platform. At first, the ball is moving sideways. Gravity pulls it downward, so its path curves toward the ground. Now release a second ball from the same height at exactly the same moment. Give this second ball no sideways push. Both balls begin with zero vertical velocity, and gravity changes their downward motion in the same way. Ignoring air resistance, they reach the ground together. The launched ball travels farther sideways, but its horizontal motion does not delay its fall. Gravity acts throughout the flight.
